A team's tasks in three columns, each with a Status's dot by its title and its count. Drag a card, within its column or to another, with a mouse from anywhere on it, or on a touch screen after a long press; or tab to its handle, press Space, move it with the arrow keys, and press Space again. Its menu, which shows as you point at the card, moves it too, and deletes it. Each card links to its own page. Done is empty, so it shows an Empty State.

To pick up a card, press Space or Enter on its handle. Move it with the arrow keys, within its column or to another, then press Space or Enter to drop it, or Escape to put it back. Its Actions menu moves it too.

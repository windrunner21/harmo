A button with aria-haspopup="menu" opens a list of actions, each a <button role="menuitem">, with an optional icon. Opened with Enter, Space, or ↓, focus goes to the first; the arrows move it, and typing a letter goes to the item that starts with it. Choosing one closes the menu. Delete is data-destructive.

An item with aria-haspopup="menu" and popovertargetaction="show" opens a menu beside it, a Dropdown Menu inside this one, after its item. Pointed at, it opens a moment later; resting on another item closes it, so the pointer can cross others on its way. →, Enter, or Space opens it on its first item, and ← closes it. Choosing in it closes both.

Its items can be links, each an <a role="menuitem">. Opened from the end of a bar, such as a header's round Create, it's aligned to its button's end with the Popup's data-align="end".

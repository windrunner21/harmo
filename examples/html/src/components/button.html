Primary for the main action, secondary for the others, and tertiary for the least prominent. data-size="small": 29px tall with 13px text, for notices, table rows, and cards. disabled dims a button and takes it out of the tab order. aria-disabled="true" looks the same but stays focusable, so a tooltip can say why: Tab reaches the last button here. data-destructive, for an action that destroys something, turns any variant red: filled, outlined with red text, or red text alone. The more an action destroys, the more weight its button gets, as in a workspace's danger zone, here a raised Card around a Settings list. The focus ring keeps the accent.

In a narrow container, drawn dashed here, a long label wraps and the button grows taller instead of overflowing. aria-busy with aria-disabled keeps a loading button focusable, and a spinner follows its label or takes its icon's place. Press the last button to watch it load for two seconds. data-hold asks for a hold before an action that's hard to undo. Hold it for 1.5 seconds, with the pointer or with Enter or Space: its fill, a copy of its content in .hbutton-hold, wipes across as you do, stays full for a moment, and the action starts; let go early and it snaps back. The fill is red on a destructive button, and the accent on any other. A screen reader presses with a click, which can't hold, so its click runs the action.

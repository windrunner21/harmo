A Kbd after the input shows the shortcut that reaches the field, here Ctrl K, or ⌘K on Apple devices. aria-keyshortcuts on the input names it, the Kbd is hidden from screen readers, and the app handles the key itself. Once there's text, the clear button takes the Kbd's place; on a device that can't hover, there's no Kbd.

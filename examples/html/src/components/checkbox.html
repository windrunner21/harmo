--harmo-checkbox-size resizes the box, and the marks scale with it; here 24px instead of 20px.
data-size="small" on a horizontal Field, for a line of fine print, such as a sign-up form's: a 13px label and a 16px checkbox, still beside the label's first line. It sizes a Radio or a Switch the same way.
.hfield-content holds the label and a description, connected to the checkbox with aria-describedby.

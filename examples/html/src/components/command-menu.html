A Search field in a dashboard's header: a click, typing, or / opens the list of places to go to and things to create. Typing filters them by name, by where they are, and by words that mean the same, such as "billing" for Plans, and marks what matched. From two letters, the app looks for customers too: the list says it's searching, then shows what it found as it sends it, with a way to see them all. Enter goes to the highlighted one, and the field empties. A shortcut that's a key alone needs a way to turn it off, as WCAG asks: here, the switch.

A website's search, from a button that looks like a Search field: it, or ⌘K (Ctrl K other than on Apple devices), opens a dialog near the top of the window, with the field and the whole list. The list filters as you type, as in the header. Enter goes to the highlighted page and closes the dialog; Escape or a click outside closes it without going anywhere. Each opening starts afresh.

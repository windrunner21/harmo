A dashboard's navigation: links in groups, each an icon and its label, with a count such as unread messages, between a header and a footer. aria-current="page" marks the page you're on; here a click moves it, as your router would. A link with links under it is a <details> that opens them, with no script, each group on its own, as here, or one at a time, as in the next example. The toggle on the edge collapses it to its icons, each naming itself in a tooltip; your app keeps whether it's collapsed.

With regions, the popup is a panel: the regions in columns, each with its flag, its name, and its languages beside one another under it. The region in use has a check in its flag's place. The button shows the region too, with the language in parentheses, since a language can be in several. Left and Right move between languages, as Up and Down do, and a search would find a language by its region's name.

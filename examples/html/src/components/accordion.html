Questions and their answers, one open at a time: the items share a name, so opening one closes the one that was open, as the browser does for <details>. A click, or Enter or Space on a question, opens it and closes it, with no script. In Chrome and Firefox, finding text on the page opens the answer it's in.
How am I billed?

Each month, for the conversations your team handled that month. Your plan's conversations come first, and any beyond them are billed at your plan's rate.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes, at any time, in Settings under Plans. A bigger plan starts at once; a smaller one starts with your next month.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your workspace stays as it is, but you can't send messages until you choose a plan. Nothing is deleted.
